Share Anything

Browse and share media with ease.

Share Anything is an Android utility designed to help you browse supported media on your device, inspect media associated with shared links, and share selected content with other applications.

Simple by design. Share Anything is built around local media access, link-based media discovery, and Android's standard sharing features.

What Share Anything Does

Browse Media

View supported photos, videos, audio files, and other media available on your device in a familiar file-style interface.

Inspect Shared Links

Share a supported web link with Share Anything and the app can inspect the page for available media resources such as images, video, or audio.

Share Content

Select the media you want and send it to compatible apps using Android's standard sharing features.

Privacy

Share Anything is designed to keep local files on your device unless you intentionally choose to share them elsewhere.

For more details about how the app handles files, links, network requests, and third-party services, review the Privacy Policy.
