Share Anything

Browse and share media with ease.

Share Anything is an Android utility designed to help you browse supported media on your device, inspect media from shared links, and quickly share selected content with other apps.

What Share Anything Does

Browse Media

View supported photos, videos, audio files, and other media available on your device.

Process Shared Links

Share supported web links with the app and review media resources associated with those pages.

Share Content

Select media and send it to compatible apps using Android's standard sharing features.
